# Known issues

Things we already know about. No need to report these, but a 👍 on the linked issue (when there is one) tells us how much it matters to you.

## Paper display

- **Diagrams drawn in TikZ or PGFPlots aren't drawn yet.** They're shown as a placeholder. Tap it, or switch to **PDF view**, to see the original.
- **Some very custom LaTeX** (unusual packages, author-made macros) can leave a formula or table unrendered. Report these: they're the most useful reports we get.
- **A few papers only publish a PDF**, with no LaTeX source. Those open in PDF view.

Nothing yet.
