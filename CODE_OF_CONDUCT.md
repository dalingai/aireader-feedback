# Code of conduct

This is a small, friendly space for people who read papers. Keep it that way:

- Be respectful and assume good faith, toward other users, the maintainers and the authors of the papers being discussed.
- Stay on topic: aireader, reading papers, and making both better.
- No harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, spam or advertising.
- Don't post other people's personal information, or papers and material you don't have the right to share.

We may edit, hide or remove posts and block accounts that break these rules. To report a problem privately, email [contact@dalingai.com](mailto:contact@dalingai.com).
