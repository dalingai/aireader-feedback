## Reporting a paper that looks wrong

aireader converts each paper from its LaTeX source on your device. Most papers come out clean, but LaTeX is endlessly creative, and every report helps the converter get better for everyone.

The single most useful thing is the **arXiv ID** (e.g. `2310.06825`) or link. With it we can reproduce exactly what you saw. Add a screenshot and a sentence on what's wrong, and that's a perfect report.

The reader's **⋯** menu has **Report a Problem**, which opens the form with the paper and app version already filled in.

Please don't upload paper files. The ID is all we need, and papers belong to their authors.

## What happens next

- We read everything. Reports get labels so you can follow along: `display`, `bug`, `needs-info`, `planned`, `fixed-in-next-version`.
- When a fix ships, we close the issue with the version it's in.
- Popular ideas rise to the top of [Ideas](https://github.com/dalingai/aireader-feedback/discussions/categories/ideas); we can't build everything, but we read every one.

## Good to know

- **This repository has no code.** aireader is closed source; this is only the public place for feedback.
- **Everything here is public.** Don't post personal information, private or unpublished papers, or anything you wouldn't want indexed by search engines.
- Be kind; see the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
